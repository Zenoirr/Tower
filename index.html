HOW TO USE

Open a floor and view its details.

Each row shows the most important floor information. Click it to expand the full Boss, HP, resistances, affinities and loadout section.

LOADOUTS

Check the loadout directly on the floor.

Floors can have separate Trait and Traitless loadouts. Use {floor}.png for Trait and {floor}-traitless.png for Traitless. Missing loadouts display No Loadout Detected.
