LEADERBOARD

Regarding leaderboard placements for Tower, after being cross-verified with the game developers, placements are first decided by the highest floor cleared. If multiple players tie on a cleared floor, the tie is decided by total damage dealt, since players can damage farm by overkilling the stage enemies' HP bars.

TRAITED & TRAITLESS

Both Traited and Traitless share the same floor parameters. Roadblock floors will be highlighted in red and tagged according to whether they are a Traited or Traitless roadblock. In most cases, the smalls are what make a room harder, while at other times certain boss modifiers are the main difficulty.

Floors can have separate Trait and Traitless loadouts. Use {floor}.png for Trait and {floor}-traitless.png for Traitless. Missing loadouts display No Loadout Detected.
